On the line with Callvi

A learner asks how to find the cost of one snack packet. Hear Callvi teach the calculation and offer a second explanation.

Call transcript

Callvi
Vanakkam. I am Callvi, an AI learning companion. Shall we continue in Tamil or English?
Caller
English please. I want to learn how to work out the cost of one snack packet. Could you teach me with a small example?
Callvi
So, let’s try a simple example. Imagine you buy a bundle of five snack packets for 50 rupees. To find the cost of just one, you divide 50 by five. That makes it 10 rupees per packet. Does that make sense, or should I try another example?

Captured from a live Sarvam browser call with a synthetic test caller. These excerpts include the opening, the learner’s question and the agent’s worked example later in the same call. The transcript uses standard spelling.
